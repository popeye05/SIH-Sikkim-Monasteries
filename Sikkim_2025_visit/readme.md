We, team of 4/6 ended up visiting Sikkim, We chose Gangtok, for the visit, as it consists most number of Monasteries.
#### We travelled 700km. 
Because, The internet wasnt really helping, We really do have a very little information about Monasteries. So it was important, that we see the problem with our very own eyes, because, I along with my team , believes in practicabilty, So we made a route plan, it did cover the following:
1. Enchey
2. Tsuklakhang/ King's Palace
3. Namgyul Institute of Tibetology
4. Rumtek Monastery
5. Lingdum Monastery

Enchey is the oldest Gompha, in Gangtok. And there was a monk, with whom we had a 40 mins long talk, about Monastic Culture, Education, Disciplines. 
The Audio clip isnt uploaded (as the person didnt wanted us to share it)

Next we went to Kings palace, The videography and Photography wasnt allowed but we had the permission for the educational Purposes. Here we learnt the most, About Sikkim rather than Monasteries, and most importantly what makes Sikkim Different than other states and how the 4 dynansties play a huge role, and the importance of Rumtek.
